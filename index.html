Materials

Surfaces you will touch every day

Five materials we work in, or bring your own. Tell us your budget and we will match the surface to it.

Quartz

Non-porous and consistent. Our first pick for busy family worktops and waterfall islands.

Low maintenance

Granite

Natural stone that takes heat and differs slab to slab. Looks best on stepped islands.

Heat resistant

Marble

Soft veining with plenty of character, for statement islands, vanities and bars.

Statement

Melamine

Durable carcasses and doors in woodgrains, solid colours and gloss. Good value.

Best value

Postform

Rolled-edge laminate tops that are practical, affordable and easy to wipe clean.

How it works

From your floor plan to the first dinner

Every job follows the same four steps, with one team from your first message to the handover.

  1. 01

    Share your plan

    Send your floor plan and budget on WhatsApp or email, or visit the showroom.

  2. 02

    Design & quote

    We measure, plan around your appliances and give you materials and a clear quote.

  3. 03

    Crafted in-house

    Our carpenters cut, edge and assemble every carcass and door to your measurements.

  4. 04

    Fitted & finished

    We travel across Zimbabwe to install and seal, then hand over a kitchen ready to cook in.

FAQ

Questions we get asked most

What services do you offer?

We design, plan and install fitted kitchens, bedrooms, bathrooms and built-in cupboards (BICs), including bars, counters and custom pieces.

Will you work with my appliances?

Usually, yes. Send your appliance sizes with your plan and we will design around them.

Which materials can I choose from?

Melamine, granite, quartz, marble and postform, plus custom material requests.

Can I supply my own materials?

You can. Please check with our team first so we can confirm the material suits the design and the fitting method.

Do you work outside Harare?

Yes. Our team fits kitchens, bedrooms and bathrooms across Zimbabwe. Tell us your location and we will plan the install.

When can I visit the showroom?

Monday to Friday 9:00 to 16:00, Saturday 8:00 to 13:00. Private appointments, holidays included, on request.
